## How this island works

Hi, I'm Christian Cortez. This island is my portfolio turned into a small world: every workbench on the plaza ring opens one role or project.

If you want the fastest path, start with the four benches either side of the north gate, the ones the guide marks **Case study**: TaskForge, EngineX, QuantLab and the LiDAR research. Each runs a small live model of the work. Crash a TaskForge worker, send an order through EngineX, re-run a QuantLab backtest, or watch a LiDAR scan pass. Picking any bench in the recruiter guide takes you straight to it.

If you have a little more time, the rest of the world adds context around how I think, how I collaborate, and what kinds of environments I care about building.

New to the controls? Walk with **WASD** or the arrow keys, hold **Shift** to run, and press **E** near a workbench to open it. On larger screens, the **How to play** button in the top corner lists everything.

Prefer reading? The same work is written up as case studies, with a downloadable resume, at [ccortez.com](https://ccortez.com).
