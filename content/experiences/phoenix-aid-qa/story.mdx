## Backend and AI for Clinical-Trial Data

<ExtractionNote />

Phoenix-Aid's AccuraTrials platform helps research teams build and run clinical studies. Setting up a study means turning a long protocol document into an electronic case report form (eCRF), and that first pass used to take most of a working day.

I built NestJS/TypeScript REST APIs and versioned PostgreSQL schemas for the platform, and kept p95 latency under 300 ms across more than 20,000 staging requests.

My main project was an asynchronous LLM extraction pipeline that drafts eCRF study builds from protocols. It runs on Redis-backed queues with retries and validates its output before anyone sees it. Together with the rest of the workflow, it helped cut first-pass study setup from eight hours to 40 minutes.

Because model output can be wrong in quiet ways, I measured it: 91% exact match against 1,200 human-reviewed protocol elements, and 94% validity across 300 generated edit checks. I also wrote more than 250 automated tests for the platform, reaching 86% coverage.
