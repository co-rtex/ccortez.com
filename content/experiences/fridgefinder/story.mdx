## FridgeFinder

FridgeFinder started as a practical question: how do you turn a loose list of ingredients into something useful, fast, and personalized? I built the project as a full-stack AI recipe platform rather than a simple frontend demo.

On the backend, I used FastAPI to expose REST endpoints that parse ingredients and route them into OpenAI-powered recipe generation flows. I paired that with normalized PostgreSQL schemas for users and recipes so the product could persist results cleanly and scale without collapsing into ad hoc storage.

On the frontend side, I built the React experience around recipe discovery and nutrition context. I also containerized the stack so deployment stayed reproducible instead of depending on one local development machine.

What makes this project valuable to me is that it ties together API design, AI integration, data modeling, UI work, and deployment discipline into one coherent product.
