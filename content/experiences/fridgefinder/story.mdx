## FridgeFinder

FridgeFinder started as a practical question: how do you turn a loose list of ingredients into a meal you can cook tonight?

It is a front-end prototype. You list what is in your fridge, get a set of recipe suggestions, open one to see its ingredients and nutrition facts, and follow it in a cook mode with a timer for each step. The suggestions come from sample data, so the prototype shows the experience rather than the generation behind it.

I built it in React and TypeScript with Vite, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui components. Working through the screens first made the open question concrete: what a recipe generator would have to return for each of them to work.
