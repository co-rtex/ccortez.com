# Technical Computing at SSEC

The Space Science and Engineering Center at UW–Madison works on weather, atmospheric, and space-science research, and its datasets are measured in terabytes. When a processing run fails partway through, a researcher loses hours.

As a Technical Computing Engineer, I automated the command-line and data-pipeline workflows that researchers used to run by hand. The work was in Python and Bash on Linux, close to the people who depended on it: more than 15 researchers.

The goal was runs that finish the first time. First-pass pipeline completion rose to 90–95%, and the manual overhead around each run fell by about 30%.

SSEC taught me that reliable infrastructure is often quiet work. Good automation does not look impressive in a demo; it shows up as researchers spending their time on research.
