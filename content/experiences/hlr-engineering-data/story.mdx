## Engineering Workflows Through a Data Lens

My time at HLR Engineering sat at the intersection of engineering operations, automation, and reporting. The work was practical: reduce manual effort, improve data accuracy, and make recurring workflows easier to trust.

I used Python to automate cost estimation and compliance reporting, which reduced both manual time and opportunities for error. That kind of work taught me how valuable even small workflow improvements can be when they are applied to repetitive operational processes.

I also worked with CAD and GIS-adjacent workflows, helping digitize processes that had previously been slower and more cumbersome. The result was faster turnaround and more accurate reporting.

This experience helped me see that engineering value does not only come from building new products. It also comes from improving the systems professionals already rely on every day.
