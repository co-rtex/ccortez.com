## Finance Ops Reconciliation Automation

This project focused on a classic operational pain point: reconciling data across multiple sources without burying people in manual review work. I built the pipeline around traceability, validation, and repeatable exception handling.

Using Python, Pandas, SQL, and PostgreSQL, I structured reconciliation workflows that compare datasets, flag mismatches, and reduce the amount of repetitive manual checking required to reach confidence in the output.

I also emphasized auditability. The system produces validation checks and audit-trail reports so decisions are explainable after the fact, not just during development.

The outcome I care about most is that it demonstrates operational engineering discipline. It is not just data processing. It is a controlled workflow that teams can trust.
