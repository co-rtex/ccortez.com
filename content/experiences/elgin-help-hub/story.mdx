## Elgin Help Hub

Elgin Help Hub was built around accessibility and usefulness. The goal was to help people quickly reach local aid resources through a responsive interface instead of forcing them through scattered, inconsistent listings.

I built the platform with React and REST services, then structured ingestion workflows to standardize community listings so the data model could support reliability and future expansion.

What matters most to me in this project is the human side of the engineering. It was not enough to display data. The platform needed to be fast, understandable, and trustworthy for people trying to find real services.

It represents a version of product engineering that is both technical and community-minded.
