## HCSC Automation Team

<HcscReconcileNote />

At HCSC I joined the Automation Team for the summer and worked on a recurring cross-team bottleneck: confirming that check records in SQL Server matched what a payment-status service reported.

I built a C#/.NET service with Entity Framework that reconciles those records against the payment-status REST API and flags mismatches. For the 8–15 internal UAT users who tested it, verifying a record went from 5–10 minutes of manual checking to under a minute.

Because the service depends on another system, I spent real effort on failure behavior: validation, timeouts, retries, cancellation, and clear fallback messages when the upstream call is slow or down. Queries are parameterized and backed by a composite index, and 20–30 unit and integration tests caught edge cases before production. Logging cut the time it took to diagnose a problem by 50–70%.

It was a good lesson in how much of a tool's value comes from how it behaves on a bad day.
